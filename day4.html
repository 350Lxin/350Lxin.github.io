An Evening at the College Fair

Read the story once. Then listen again and shadow the news-style rhythm.

On Friday afternoon, Daniel arrived at his college after a short online class. He had a simple scheme: study in the library, join a friendly tennis game, and watch the evening firework show. Before he left home, he checked his phone bill and made sure the minimum payment had been made. He also confirmed his dinner reservation online.

周五下午，Daniel 上完一节在线课后到达了学院。他有一个简单的计划：在图书馆学习、参加一场友好的网球比赛，然后观看晚上的焰火表演。出门前，他查看了电话账单，确认最低应付款已经支付。

At the library, Daniel entered through the side door. The librarian asked for his identification and a signature. The old card catalogue helped him find a book from the library's rare collection about literature. He also used an online reference to check the source of a story. The librarian made a photocopy for him, but the printer stopped when a cable came loose.

在图书馆，图书管理员要求 Daniel 出示身份证明并签名。旧式卡片目录帮他找到了文学书籍。他还通过在线参考资料核对文章的出处。图书管理员为他复印了一份资料，但打印机因为一根电缆松动而停止工作。

An engineer came to fix it, while the supervisor gave the staff clear guidance. The repair was completed automatically after a new program was installed. The librarian said the rule was to keep the reading room quiet, but the hallway outside was becoming noisy. Daniel moved to the room on the opposite side of the building.

一名工程师来修理打印机，主管给工作人员提供了清晰的指导。安装新程序后，修理过程自动完成了。图书管理员说，规则是保持阅览室安静，但外面的走廊开始变得吵闹。Daniel 走到了大楼另一侧的房间。

That evening, the college held a small science fair. Its theme was life in a tropical sea. One display showed a coral reef, and another used a camera to record a harmless snake moving through grass. A Canadian student explained that her nationality was Canadian, and that she had learned about the reef during a summer project.

那天晚上，学院举办了一场小型科学展览，主题是热带海洋中的生命。一个展台展示了珊瑚礁，另一个展台用摄像机记录一条无害的蛇穿过草地。一名加拿大留学生解释说，她的国籍是加拿大人，她在暑期项目中了解了这片礁区。

After the fair, students changed into a sports uniform. Daniel played baseball first, then practiced tennis with a new racket. The coach said exercise would benefit everyone, but the wet court meant they had to transfer the game indoors. The sports service desk gave each player a towel and a bottle of water.

展览结束后，学生们换上运动制服。Daniel 先打棒球，然后用新网球拍练习网球。教练说锻炼对每个人都有好处，但潮湿的场地让他们不得不把比赛转移到室内。运动服务台给每位球员一条毛巾和一瓶水。

For dinner, the manager welcomed everyone to a buffet. Daniel preferred rice and vegetables, and the waiter explained the amount of food on each plate. A dessert stayed cold in the freezer. The meal cost less than expected, so the students could collect money for a library project. The only extra expense was a small deposit for a room key. Daniel put the key in his pocket and promised to hold it safely.

晚餐时，经理欢迎大家来到自助餐厅。Daniel 更喜欢米饭和蔬菜，服务员说明了每个盘子里的食物量。这顿饭比预想的便宜，所以学生们可以为图书馆项目筹集资金。唯一额外的开支是房间钥匙的小额押金。Daniel 把钥匙放进口袋，保证会保管好。

Later, Daniel paid for the meal by card. The bank recorded the transaction, and the financial office sent him a receipt. He noticed that his student card would expire next month, so he made a note to renew it. Outside, a camera lens began to zoom toward the bright sky. The students adjusted their trousers, watched the fireworks, and finally found a quiet place for relaxation.

后来，Daniel 用卡支付了餐费。银行记录了这笔交易，财务办公室给他发了收据。他注意到学生卡下个月到期，于是记下了续卡的提醒。外面，一台摄像机的镜头开始向明亮的天空推进。学生们整理好长裤，观看焰火，最后找了一个安静的地方放松。
