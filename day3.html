A Small Festival by the Sea

On Saturday morning, Mia helped her cousin Leo prepare for the town festival. Their primary plan was simple: arrive early, help the team, and enjoy the music. Leo put a reusable bottle and some fruit in the fridge. Mia checked the event registration on her tablet and saw that each participant needed one single ticket.

星期六早上，Mia 帮助表兄 Leo 为小镇节日做准备。他们的主要计划很简单：早点到达、帮助团队、享受音乐。Leo 把可重复使用的水瓶和水果放进冰箱。Mia 在平板电脑上查看活动登记信息，发现每位参加者都需要一张单程票。

They drove along the wide avenue to the community center. Before leaving, Leo parked the car in a nearby garage. At the entrance, a volunteer checked Leo's driving license, passport, and postcode. All the details were valid, so the volunteer gave him a blue certificate for the cooking class. A small advertisement beside the desk announced an additional art lesson.

他们沿着宽阔的大街开到社区中心。在入口处，一名志愿者检查了 Leo 的驾照、护照和邮编。所有信息都有效，因此志愿者给了他一张烹饪课证书。桌旁的一则小广告还介绍了一堂额外的艺术课。

Before the festival opened, the center had a few problems. A technician came to repair a speaker, while Mia helped handle the chairs. Leo pushed a guest in a wheelchair onto the wooden deck. Near the pool, a worker used a vacuum to clean the floor. The only concern was the hot weather, but the advantage of the center was its cool shade. The disadvantage was that the old building needed more repair.

节日开始前，中心出现了几个问题。技术员来修理音箱，Mia 帮忙处理椅子。Leo 推着一位坐轮椅的客人走上木制甲板。游泳池旁，一名工作人员用吸尘器清洁地面。唯一的担忧是天气炎热，但中心的优势是有阴凉处。劣势是这座老建筑还需要更多维修。

At noon, families sat together for dining. The cook explained that rice, bread, and beans were healthy sources of carbohydrate. He added a little salt and asked everyone to share the food. Mia made a small purchase at the craft stall: a bowl made from clay. She received a discount, and the bowl had an original blue design with a purple bird.

中午，家人们一起吃饭。厨师解释说，米饭、面包和豆类是健康的碳水化合物来源。他加了一点盐，并请大家分享食物。Mia 买了一个黏土碗，还享受了折扣。这个碗有原创的蓝色图案，上面画着一只紫色的鸟。

In the afternoon, an artist showed children how to draw a medial line through a shape. Then the children made clay boats and placed them on the water. A short race started beside the swimming pool. The center also offered a friendly information service for visitors who needed help. After that, the piano player performed a gentle song. The local radio broadcast the music, and the audience clapped. The main feature of the day was that everyone could join, including people with different jobs and different kinds of occupation.

下午，一位艺术家教孩子们如何在图形中画一条中线。孩子们制作黏土小船，把它们放在水面上。游泳池旁开始了一场短跑。随后，钢琴演奏者演奏了一首温柔的歌曲。当地电台播出了音乐，观众纷纷鼓掌。这一天的主要特点是每个人都能参加，包括从事不同职业的人。

As evening came, a candle lit the table near the tent. Mia read a little poetry about the sea. Leo helped arrange the final concert and gave a detailed thank-you speech. He said the first, or initial, plan had changed many times, but the team stayed calm. One child lost a toy, but the loss was soon solved. When the alarm rang, everyone left through the entrance. They still had one opportunity to return next year, and the happy memory would remain.

傍晚，一支蜡烛照亮了帐篷旁的桌子。Mia 读了一小段关于大海的诗歌。Leo 帮忙安排最后的音乐会，并发表了详细的感谢致辞。他说最初的计划改了很多次，但团队始终保持冷静。有个孩子丢了玩具，不过损失很快就解决了。警报响起后，大家从入口离开。他们明年仍有机会回来，而这段快乐的记忆会一直留在心中。
